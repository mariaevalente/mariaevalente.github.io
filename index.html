Research, 2025–2026

Predictive HVAC control

A controller that forecasts every zone 30 minutes ahead and, every 15 minutes, picks the least energy-intensive action that keeps the occupied zones comfortable. I tested it on ten simulated buildings against a fixed schedule and a rule-based controller.

−26%HVAC energy against fixed schedules, and less discomfort than the rule-based controller in all ten buildings

The Valente chart

I designed this view to show, on one time axis, what the equipment did and how the room responded: running periods coloured by mode, the setpoint and the room temperature. The team calls it the Valente chart, and it closes every monthly client report.

Patent and talks, 2022–2026

Patent and synthetic data

A new building has no useful history to learn from. I proposed pre-training the forecasting models on synthetic data from the building's digital twin, so that control works from the first day. It is now an international patent application, and I presented the approach at Ai4 in Las Vegas.

Platform, 2025–2026

A simulation platform for any building

One TOML file describes a building: zones, comfort ranges, opening hours, equipment and its command map. Simulation, data generation, training, the controllers and the checks all read it, so a new building is a new file instead of new code.

About

I'm a computer engineer from Viana do Castelo. I studied at the University of Minho, specialising in data science and intelligent systems, and did my master's research at UCL on adversarial deep learning for network anomaly detection.

I joined Bandora in 2021 as a data scientist and have led its R&D since May 2026. My work sits between machine learning, building physics and software engineering, and I like carrying an idea from the first experiment to something a client uses.

What I bring

  • Ideas taken all the way, from the first experiment to something a client uses.
  • Evaluation I can defend: baselines, measurement and verification, and honest comparisons.
  • Tools other people use: a language for control rules and a reporting platform.
  • Clear communication across teams: I explain technical work to people without a technical background, so they understand it and can act on it, whether in a meeting, a client report or the documentation.

Beyond the code

I set up the team's engineering standards, mentor interns and master's students, and give talks inside and outside the company.
