Analytics and reporting platform

What it does

For one building or a whole portfolio, the application shows what the controller did, the comfort it held, what the building consumed and saved, and how complete the data behind those numbers is. At the end of the month it writes the same content into a single HTML report for the client, closing with the Valente chart for every zone.

13 s → 3 msfor a filtered time-series read, after I traced full collection scans and specified a compound index with the data team
4× faster reportsThe monthly report took almost a minute to open. After I reworked the database queries and indexes, it opens in under 14 seconds.

Rules behind the numbers

  • A month's saving only counts when the building and its reference cover the same stretch of time. Months still in progress, or with a meter installed halfway, are shown but left out, with the reason printed next to them.
  • Comfort is weighted by time and judged against each weekday's own opening hours.
  • Every read that fails is reported on the page, so a broken query never looks like an empty building.

Packaging

The application ships as a multi-stage Docker image that runs as a non-root user. The build checks its imports and assets before it finishes, the container has a health check, and its port is bound to localhost behind an authenticating proxy.
