The Valente chart

A view I designed to show, on one time axis, what the equipment did and how the room responded. Here it runs on a full simulated year for three buildings and three controllers.

Reading it

The shaded bands are the time the units serving a zone were running, coloured by mode. The dotted band is the comfort range, drawn only during opening hours. The orange line is the room, and the cyan step line is the setpoint the controller chose.

It opens on 5 November in a restaurant kitchen. The predictive controller keeps the kitchen in range all day with the fan alone. The fixed schedule heats all day for the same temperatures and uses about twice the energy. The rule-based controller cycles the cooling and leaves the kitchen above range for half its opening hours.

Other days worth a look

Try a week in August, the medium office, or the retail building in summer. In that building no controller can hold the comfort range in July and August, and the chart shows it as plainly as it shows a good day.
