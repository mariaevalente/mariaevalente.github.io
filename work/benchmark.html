Why it matters

In a contract paid from savings, the baseline is what gets invoiced. The question is what the building would have used without the controller, and how sure we can be of the answer.

The approach

I started with a climate-corrected baseline that relates consumption to heating and cooling degree-hours. It grew into a tournament. Five inverse change-point models are fitted to at least a year of consumption against outdoor temperature, and the most accurate model that meets CV(RMSE) ≤ 15% and |NMBE| ≤ 5% becomes the building's benchmark.
