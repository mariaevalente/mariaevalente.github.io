Research, 2025–2026

Predictive HVAC control

A controller that forecasts every zone 30 minutes ahead and, every 15 minutes, picks the least energy-intensive action that keeps the occupied zones comfortable.

I tested it on ten simulated buildings, six ASHRAE prototypes and four digital twins of real buildings, over a full year. Each building ran under a fixed schedule, a rule-based controller and the predictive controller, with the same weather, occupancy and constraints.

−25.6%energy against the fixed schedule
−46%discomfort against the rule-based controller
6.4×fewer candidate actions per decision in the second generation
0.35 °Cclosed-loop forecast error, 30 minutes ahead

Role Technical lead and main developer. The decision method is proprietary and is shown only through its results.

The Valente chart

The view I designed to show what the equipment did and how the room responded, on one time axis: running periods coloured by mode, the comfort range during opening hours, the setpoint and the room temperature. It closes every monthly client report.

Measurement and verification, 2023–2026

Savings benchmark

When a contract is paid from savings, the baseline is what gets invoiced. I set up the climate-corrected baseline and supervised its move to a tournament of ASHRAE Guideline 14 change-point models: the most accurate one meeting CV(RMSE) ≤ 15% and |NMBE| ≤ 5% becomes the benchmark.

31 of 32buildings with a compliant model
5.6%median CV(RMSE) of the models selected

Patent and talks, 2022–2026

Patent and synthetic data

WO 2026/033501 A1

Method for controlling HVAC equipment of a building, computer system and computer program product thereof

Inventors
Maria Elisa Maciel Valente, R. Teixeira, G. Karafotias, M. Pereira
Priority
9 August 2024
Published
12 February 2026

A new building has no useful history to learn from. The method pre-trains the energy and temperature forecasters on synthetic data from the building's digital twin, combined with whatever was measured, so that control works from the first day.

It grew out of a 2022 warm-up optimiser, XGBoost forecasts searched with simulated annealing, that cut HVAC cost inside the morning warm-up window by 27 to 45% in simulation. I presented the approach at Ai4 2024 in Las Vegas and at AI Nest 2026 in Coimbra.

Role First inventor.

Platform, 2025–2026

A simulation platform for any building

One TOML file describes a building: zones, comfort ranges, opening hours, equipment and its command map. Simulation, data generation, training, the controllers and the checks all read it, so a new building is a new file instead of new code. 71 buildings and archetypes use the format. I also designed the team's reproducibility workflow with DVC, MLflow and Git.

Role Spec schema, API layer and sanity tests. Testbed built with the R&D team.

Language design, 2023–2024

BuildFlow

if comfort_less([1, 2]) > 0:
    ACTUATION([1], [["Mode", "Cooling"]], [])
else:
    if comfort_less([1, 2]) < 0:
        ACTUATION([1], [["Mode", "Heating"]], [])
    else:
        ACTUATION([1], [["Mode", "Fan"]], []);

A small domain-specific language for expert control rules, with its own PLY parser, a compiler to JSON and an interpreter. Engineers write and review the rules, and the production rule engine runs them. Six production controllers are written in it.
